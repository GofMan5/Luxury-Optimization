# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and technically honest.
- Critique code and evidence, not people.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes and keep private information private.
- Prefer reproducible measurements over claims or marketing.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Report conduct issues privately through a GitHub Security Advisory for this repository. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with the minimum necessary disclosure.

This policy is based on Contributor Covenant 2.1: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
